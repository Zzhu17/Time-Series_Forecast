# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent and give constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or hostile behavior.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers at:
conduct@example.com

Reports will be handled privately and respectfully.
